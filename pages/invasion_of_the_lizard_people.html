Invasion of the Lizard People

Invasion of the Lizard People is a top-down multiplayer action game built entirely by me as a solo developer in Unity. I handled the full production process, including game design, programming, 3D modelling, UI, animation, visual effects, audio integration, networking, Steam integration, optimisation and release.

The game features fast-paced cooperative combat across two distinct modes. Arena, available in the current Steam release, provides focused and replayable combat encounters. Survival is a larger roguelike-inspired mode built around procedurally generated multi-biome environments, exploration, quests, bosses, loot, crafting, character progression and increasingly dangerous enemies. Survival is currently in its final testing and bug-fixing phase and is not yet included in the public Steam version.

The project uses Unity URP and Mirror networking, with a strong focus on responsive multiplayer gameplay, procedural systems, scalable enemy behaviour and building a polished game that remains practical for a solo developer to maintain.

Available now on Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/app/4621860/Invasion_of_the_Lizard_People/
